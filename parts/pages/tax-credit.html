Company incentives

Qualified Entertainment Company Tax Incentives

A Qualified Entertainment Company (QEC) gets tax credits for full time, permanent jobs. A QEC is a company directly or indirectly engaged in the distribution of audio and/or video entertainment content for public consumption. A QEC must create five new quality jobs.

15%

For each new job for wages equal to or greater than $45K/year, up to $66K per year for W2 employees

20%

Credit for each new job for wages equal to or greater than $65K per year for W2 employees

$1,000,000

Maximum amount per year of credits granted to a QEC

$200,000

Maximum qualifying individual salary per person per year
