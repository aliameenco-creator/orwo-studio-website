Highlights

Event Highlights

01

Louisiana International Film Festival

Film Festival Stage

The Louisiana International Film Festival is Louisiana’s leading annual film festival. The festival is dedicated to premiering stunning works in cinematography, music composition, software development, acting, engineering, and more.

02

Wrap Parties

An event in the end of the practical shoot.

Blaze, Jeepers Creepers, Salvage Marines, and many more productions have celebrated the end of their shoots with wrap parties hosted by ORWO Studios. We have a variety of settings which can be used, including soundstages, our on-site Bar and Grill, our movie theatre, or outside with the backdrop of our beautiful antebellum buildings and woodlands.

03

Brunch with Louisiana State Governor

State of the Nation meeting, with the Governor

The Governor of Louisiana, Governor John Bel Edwards, joined us for a tour of the facility and a four hour brunch session which included in depth discussions on the future of the industry in Louisiana.

04

Cinematography Board and Workshops

To plan, share and encourage

Louisiana has many fine crew members and a depth of local talent both in front and behind the camera. Here we featured some of the very best cinematography powerhouses, sharing their knowledge with the wider industry.
