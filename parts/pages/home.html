About ORWO Studios

"Hollywood South"

We always strive for the best

A very unique Film Studio in the heart of Louisiana, just 15 minutes from Baton Rouge airport, but in a serene and tranquil rural setting. ORWO Studios is a carefully crafted creative environment and the ideal place for film and television productions; quiet, focused and with excellent accommodation and transport links, including a private airport next door.

ORWO Studios is constantly developing and expanding. Currently the studio boasts: 3 sound stages, 35mm lab, vfx department, product placement and branding, set design and build services, full post production (Avid DS, QTake, FCP, Nuke, Speedgrade, Resolve etc), cameras, catering, antique props/furnishings, large and varied backlot, accommodation and of course: office buildings, fishing pond, antebellum backdrops.

Services

Hollywood grade film services in Louisiana

Production

Finest Louisiana Filming Locations or Sound Stages - we've got you covered.

Set support on all stages, In-house Crew, Transport, Locations, Camera equipment rental, DI equipment and QTake, 3D Stereo, VR and 360 RIGS, Hi-Speed cameras, gimbals and heavy drones, 3D Printing/Scanning, CNC prop workshop, Craft services and catering, Film production offices, costume and props rental and storage.

Standing Sets

Shooting ready sets located across our studio lot.

We have a variety of standing sets that are all shooting ready. Ranging from a true American Cafe Diner located in the heart of our studio lot, to an outlaw country treehouse buried in our backlot.

Post-production

Thanks to our unique movie production workflow we can edit as we shoot.

We can process footage in real time, on set. With mobile internet links, this is uploaded in real time to our FTP and RAID, for rushes/dailies and of course editorial. Everything is backed up daily to LTO7. Over 10 Petabyte of footage processing and storage. 10Gbit on set accessible.

CGI and VFX

Local and international talent, with review rooms in multiple cities

CGI and VFX labs with an industry class renderfarm and production pipeline. Through our strategic tie ups we have the ability to provide review in Los Angeles, London and numerous European cities.

Grading

Davinci Resolve Clipster Speedgrade

DCI Compliant Barco projection system on site, with a 30 seat cinema. Full size theater screen. 12 separate editing suits (Avid, Resolve and FCP) for grading and QC review.

Delivery

International or domestic. DCP or ONLINE services

We ensure fast delivery according to local and worldwide standards. Multilingual subtitling. DCPs can be created in real time, for same day delivery. All tape formats and file formats are catered for.

Distribution

A boutique world sales agency

Our sales company can offer a wide array of titles, all with theatrical ability in select territories, including USA and UK. From feature films and documentaries, to VOD series'. Visit orwodistribution.com to browse their 2022 titles.

35mm Lab

There is a difference between a film and a video

Our Film Lab is the only one in the world that solely specialises in producing 35mm motion picture film prints for filmmakers worldwide. We use state-of-the-art technology to print direct to positive 35mm film straight from your data source, eliminating ALL stages involving expensive negative intermediates. Producing cinema-quality prints at the most affordable prices.

Aerial Cinematography

Drones of all shapes and sizes up to 32K RAW 360

You can see shots by our experienced aerial cinematography unit in many recent movies, including Jeepers Creepers, Grand Isle, and Blaze. Ready for architectural, industrial or event flights as well.

Louisiana Tax Credit Finance

Helping productions access and maximize the Louisiana incentive

We have an in depth knowledge of the local incentives - which are alive and well. Louisiana combines deep crews, wonderful locations and a world class tax credit / film incentive system. We can cash flow it!

On-site Accommodation

A welcoming family-owned bed & breakfast

Where movie stars mix with locals, where nature surrounds you and the hospitality makes you feel at home. Steeped in history, our buildings are more than 200 years old. More than 20 self catering suites in historic and antebellum buildings on site. Accommodation for crews up to 500 in close proximity. Meeting rooms available. On-site bar & grill.

ORWO Film - Our motion picture film partner!

ORWO is a 116 year old company and brand. Founded in Wolfen, in 1909; as an off-shoot of the AGFA film company.

ORWO develops and produces colour and b/w films for cameras in the photographic and cinematic market in a century-old tradition.
