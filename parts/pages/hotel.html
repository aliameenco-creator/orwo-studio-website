ORWO Studios Hotel

A historical hotel on the famous ORWO Film Studios Lot, where movie stars mix with locals...

Immerse yourself in Antebellum luxury and Southern comfort at ORWO Studios Hotel, where nature surrounds you and the hospitality makes you feel at home. Stay on a real Film Studio lot in historic and antebellum buildings, as seen in movies including The Magnificent Seven (Denzel Washington, Chris Pratt) Blaze (Ethan Hawke), Grand Isle (Nicolas Cage), Jeepers Creepers (Jonathan Breck), and many more!

With more than 20 self-catering suites available, each room has its own story to tell...
